No kid should get sidelined by a blown derailleur or a $200 brake bleed.

Mountain biking on Vancouver Island builds grit, confidence, and community—but the cost barrier is brutal. Shred LAB CR connects youth with hands-on mechanical mentorship and a recycled gear library so every kid can keep rolling.

Donate Used Gear & Bikes Partner With Us

Hands-On Wrenching

We teach youth how their bikes actually work—from tubeless setups and drivetrain indexing to brake bleeding and suspension setup. No gatekeeping, just trailside self-reliance.

Community Gear Library

Outgrown a full-face helmet or swapped out a working 4-piston brake set? We inspect, service, and pass quality take-off parts down to local kids who need them to ride safely.

Trail Stewardship

Respecting the terrain we ride. Mentoring kids on trail etiquette, drainage maintenance, and active stewardship across our local riding networks.

The Parts We Need Most

Cleaning out your shop or garage? We actively collect and restore:

Protection Full-face helmets, knee/elbow pads, gloves (Youth & Small/Medium)
Brakes & Rotors Working hydraulic brakes, 180/203mm rotors, pads, adapters
Drivetrains 10/11/12-speed derailleurs, shifters, chains, narrow-wide chainrings
Wheels & Tires 24", 26", 27.5" wheels, functional tubeless tires with tread left

Our Operating Pillars

More Than Just Riding. Real Skills for Life.

We connect local trail culture, mechanical self-reliance, and recycled hardware right here in Campbell River.

Mechanical Mentorship

Hands-on clinic sessions where youth learn how bicycles work from the inside out: hydraulic brake bleeds, tubeless tire installs, suspension tuning, drivetrain indexing, and emergency trailside fixes.

  • Tool safety & torque specs
  • Suspension sag & damper setup
  • Earn-a-tool youth program

The Community Gear Library

We rescue quality pre-loved mountain bikes, full-face helmets, knee guards, and upgraded components. Inspected, rebuilt, and loaned to young riders whose families couldn't otherwise afford safe gravity gear.

  • Free youth protective armor loans
  • Upgraded 1x drivetrains & disc brakes
  • Zero waste: parts kept out of landfills

Progression & Stewardship

Fostering respect for Vancouver Island trails. We coach on-trail etiquette, partner with trail builders on maintenance days, and build safe progression sessions at local pump tracks and dirt jump lines.

  • Campbell River Bike Park sessions
  • Snowden Demon trail etiquette days
  • Inclusive group progression rides
